Statistical learning for population health and humanitarian data.

I am Nayeem Sarker, a statistics-trained researcher from Bangladesh with a completed M.Sc. in Statistics from Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST), and additional graduate coursework in Computer Science & Engineering. // Structured Explanation · Advanced Research Questions

Four connected questions. Deeper methodological challenges.

Each question is presented as a small mind map: a central scientific problem branching into deeper questions and possible methods. The curved connectors show how the ideas relate; they do not imply that a method or finding has already been established.

01 · BIOSTATISTICS & POPULATION HEALTH

Statistical inference under imperfect data

How can we obtain reliable estimates of population-health outcomes when data are incomplete, systematically mismeasured, selectively observed, or collected through non-random processes?

View full details ＋
DEEPER QUESTION A

When can missingness create bias that conventional imputation cannot adequately correct?

DEEPER QUESTION B

How sensitive are estimates to assumptions about missing data and measurement error?

DEEPER QUESTION C

Can uncertainty intervals remain well-calibrated when the data-generating process is only partly known?

Potential methodsMissing-data theoryMultiple imputationBayesian hierarchical modelsSensitivity analysisRobust estimation

Intellectual challenge: distinguish uncertainty that can be quantified from bias that depends on unverifiable assumptions.

02 · SPATIAL & TEMPORAL MODELING

Spatial-temporal inference and changing populations

How can we distinguish genuine changes in population outcomes from changes caused by population movement, reporting coverage, spatial aggregation, or data-collection practices?

View full details ＋
DEEPER QUESTION A

How do conclusions change when the same population is analyzed at different geographic scales?

DEEPER QUESTION B

Can models separate spatial dependence from temporal change and population composition?

DEEPER QUESTION C

How can emerging patterns be detected without mistaking reporting changes or noise for real trends?

Potential methodsSpatiotemporal hierarchical modelsSmall-area estimationChange-point detectionSpatial cross-validationSensitivity analysis

Intellectual challenge: separate changes in the underlying phenomenon from changes in how that phenomenon is observed.

03 · STATISTICAL LEARNING & AI

Generalization, interpretability and uncertainty in AI

Under what conditions can statistical learning models produce reliable predictions for populations, locations, or time periods that differ from the data used to train them?

View full details ＋
DEEPER QUESTION A

How should models be evaluated when deployment populations differ from training data?

DEEPER QUESTION B

When can greater predictive accuracy come at the cost of poorly understood uncertainty or failure modes?

DEEPER QUESTION C

Do model explanations reveal stable relationships or correlations specific to the training data?

Potential methodsDistribution-shift analysisExternal validationInterpretable MLCalibrationConformal predictionRobustness testing

Intellectual challenge: determine not only whether a model works, but why, where, and under what conditions its predictions can be trusted.

04 · RESPONSIBLE POPULATION DATA

Bias, privacy and responsible population inference

How can population-level evidence remain useful and equitable when data are unequally represented, sensitive, and generated within systems that may systematically exclude certain groups?

View full details ＋
DEEPER QUESTION A

How do selection bias and underrepresentation affect comparisons between groups?

DEEPER QUESTION B

How much analytical utility is lost when privacy-preserving techniques are applied?

DEEPER QUESTION C

How can genuine group differences be separated from differences in measurement, access, or reporting?

Potential methodsSelection-bias analysisSubgroup validationPrivacy-preserving statisticsFairness evaluationData provenanceSensitivity analysis

Intellectual challenge: examine who is represented, whose uncertainty is hidden, and who may be harmed when statistical results guide decisions.

THE UNIFYING DOCTORAL QUESTION

How can statistical learning frameworks remain reliable under incomplete data, population heterogeneity, spatial-temporal change, and uncertainty—while preserving interpretability and protecting vulnerable populations?

These are candidate research directions, not a finalized proposal. A feasible dissertation would narrow the scope around a specific scientific gap, study design, data source and supervisory expertise.

STATISTICAL MODELING · TIME SERIES

Rainfall Forecasting in Sylhet District

Research question. Can seasonal time-series structure be used to forecast district-level rainfall and characterize temporal variation in an environmental process?

View full details ＋

Approach. Implemented a SARIMA forecasting model in R, using statistical time-series methodology to represent trend and seasonal dynamics.

Research significance. This project established an early foundation for the spatio-temporal and predictive modeling direction that now motivates my doctoral interests in complex crisis data.

Methodological challenge. Forecast accuracy depends on seasonal assumptions, data length and how performance is evaluated against a suitable baseline.

PhD connection: temporal modeling · uncertainty · model validation

R SARIMA Time Series
SPATIAL ANALYSIS · GIS

Spatial Analysis of COVID-19 Transmission Risk

Research question. How does COVID-19 transmission risk vary geographically across Bangladesh, and where do spatial patterns indicate comparatively higher-risk areas?

View full details ＋

Approach. Combined district-level GIS mapping with inferential statistical analysis to examine spatial variation in transmission risk.

Research significance. The work connected statistical inference with geographic structure and provided an early basis for thinking about spatial dependence in population-level data.

Methodological challenge. Geographic variation in reported risk does not by itself establish spatial dependence, causation, or differences in underlying exposure.

PhD connection: spatial inference · scale effects · population heterogeneity

GIS Statistics Spatial Analysis
NLP · DEEP LEARNING

Bengali Math-Quest

Research question. How can deep-learning methods be applied to question answering in Bengali, an under-resourced language, using a domain-specific mathematical task?

View full details ＋

Approach. Developed a Bengali-language mathematics question-answering system using Python and TensorFlow, connecting language processing with neural modeling.

Research significance. The project provides the computational starting point for my current interest in low-resource NLP, data-efficient learning and multilingual AI.

Methodological challenge. Language and task-specific performance must be evaluated separately; an apparently fluent answer may still be mathematically incorrect.

Protection Information Management Systems

Research problem. How can sensitive, heterogeneous protection data be collected, validated, integrated and translated into reliable evidence under operational constraints across a large humanitarian programme?

Evidence and significance. The systems helped streamline reporting workflows. More importantly for doctoral research, the work exposed methodological challenges involving data quality, interoperability, missingness, privacy and decision-making under operational uncertainty.

PRIVACY · STATISTICS · GIS

Digital Privacy & Security for Children

Research question. What factors are associated with children's digital privacy and security risks, and how can those risks be examined quantitatively across populations and locations?

View full details ＋

Approach. Used statistical modeling, hypothesis testing, survey methods and GIS-based spatial analysis in research conducted at Leading University, with computational methods supporting the analysis.

Research significance. This work forms an early bridge between statistical analysis, sensitive population data and the responsible-AI questions that now sit within my doctoral research agenda.

Methodological challenge. Observed associations may reflect sampling, measurement or contextual differences and should not automatically be interpreted as causal effects.

PhD connection: selection bias · statistical inference · responsible data use

Python SPSS / STATA GIS
DATA QUALITY · DECISION SUPPORT

Field-to-Evidence Data Workflow

Research problem. How can field observations remain analytically trustworthy when data are collected offline, produced by multiple actors and subsequently used for protection decisions?

View full details ＋

Approach. Developed validation-driven forms, Python/SQL cleaning workflows, structured integrations and analytical dashboards connecting collection, quality assurance and reporting.

Research significance. The work motivates a broader methodological question at the heart of humanitarian informatics: how should statistical and machine-learning methods account for the imperfections and constraints of the systems that generate the data?

Methodological challenge. Validation rules can reduce avoidable errors, but they cannot eliminate all bias or guarantee that collected records represent the underlying population.

PhD connection: measurement processes · data provenance · uncertainty-aware analysis

Data Quality Python / SQL R Shiny Power BI

Published research in statistical analysis and forecasting.

My peer-reviewed publication examines patterns in internet use in Bangladesh and reflects my early research training in quantitative analysis, growth measurement and time-series forecasting.

PEER-REVIEWED JOURNAL ARTICLE · 2022

Forecast, Trend, Growth, and Instability of Internet Users in Bangladesh

Sorkar, M. N. U., Sorkar, M. R. U., Uddin, M. T., & Sarker, N. (2022). Computer Engineering and Intelligent Systems, 13(4).

DOI: 10.7176/CEIS/13-4-01

Research focus

The study examines internet-user trends, growth and instability in Bangladesh and uses time-series forecasting to explore the trajectory of internet use.

Analytical approach

The paper combines quantitative trend and growth analysis with ARIMA-based forecasting. These methods provide a foundation for thinking critically about temporal patterns, model assumptions and uncertainty in forecasts.

Connection to my doctoral interests

This work helped shape my interest in statistical modeling and evidence from real-world data. My proposed doctoral direction builds on that foundation by asking how inference and prediction can remain reliable when data are incomplete, populations change, or model assumptions are uncertain.
