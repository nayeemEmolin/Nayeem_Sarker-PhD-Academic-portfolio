04 · Proposed research

A question for future independent work

This is a proposed project direction, not a claim of completed research.

Proposed reproducible study

How Missing Data and Measurement Error Affect Population-Risk Estimates

Research question: how do different patterns of missingness and measurement error affect population-level estimates and the predictive performance of statistical models?

Potential approach: use an ethically shareable public or synthetic dataset; define controlled missingness scenarios; compare suitable complete-case and imputation approaches; evaluate bias, uncertainty, calibration, and predictive performance; and report sensitivity analyses.

Planned outputs: a research brief, reproducible code, documented data provenance, figures and tables, and a transparent discussion of limitations.

Missing-data methodsReproducible researchStatistical modelingSensitivity analysis
